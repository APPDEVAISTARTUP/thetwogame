THE WORLD

IMPORTANT

This game DOES NOT take place years after civilization has already collapsed.

It takes place DURING the outbreak.

Players experience the beginning of humanity's collapse.

The infection has only recently appeared.

Nobody fully understands it.

Governments are trying to contain it.

Military checkpoints still exist.

Hospitals are overwhelmed.

News channels are still broadcasting.

Police are attempting to maintain order.

Helicopters fly above cities.

Emergency sirens echo throughout towns.

Traffic jams cover highways.

Families are trying to escape.

People are hiding inside buildings.

Nobody knows who is infected.

Nobody knows who will survive.

The player witnesses civilization collapsing in real time.

This creates far more tension than a traditional post-apocalyptic world.
